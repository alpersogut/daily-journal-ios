# 📔 Günlük — Daily Journal iOS App

A personal journaling app for iOS built with Swift and Xcode. Günlük lets you capture your daily memories — the good and the frustrating — and look back on them whenever you want.

## ✨ Features

- **One Entry Per Day** — Each day allows a single journal entry, keeping your reflections focused and intentional.
- **Rich Media Support** — Attach as many photos and videos as you like to bring your memories to life.
- **Voice Recording** — Record audio notes directly within your daily entry.
- **Written Notes** — Add a text description to capture your thoughts and feelings.
- **🔥 Streak Tracking** — A flame-based streak system tracks your journaling consistency. Maintain a streak of 3 or more consecutive days to keep your flame alive!

## 🛠 Tech Stack

- **Language:** Swift
- **IDE:** Xcode
- **Platform:** iOS
- **Networking:** Custom `NetworkManager` for API communication
